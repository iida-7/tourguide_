## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an authoritative, cultivated editorial environment tailored for discerning travelers, cultural researchers, and travel journalists validating European itineraries. The atmosphere channels high-end European print journalism—such as architectural quarterlies and long-form travel monographs—reimagined into a precise digital validation tool.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Trustworthy & Authoritative:** The interface conveys institutional credibility, meticulous precision, and fact-checked certainty without clinical sterility.
- **Calmly Discerning:** Generous whitespace, disciplined pacing, and muted canvas tones create an unhurried, focused environment suited for rigorous verification.
- **Understated Continental Sophistication:** The aesthetic rejects flashy travel tropes, neon badges, and decorative embellishment in favor of structural clarity, fine hairline borders, and immaculate typographic hierarchy.

### Design Movement: Modern Neo-Editorial
The design system fuses modern editorial layout principles with high-clarity data architecture:
- Crisp baseline grids and asymmetric editorial columns inspired by classical continental typesetting.
- Low-contrast architectural frames using warm stone borders rather than drop shadows.
- Dedicated verification states (verified, disputed, updated, unconfirmed) that integrate seamlessly into body prose rather than feeling like jarring notification banners.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in tactile, archival warmth paired with commanding deep pigments. It eschews clinical pure whites and harsh synthetics in favor of stone-cast neutrality and dignified ink tones.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#1e3a8a` - Diplomatic Navy):** Used for focal interactive states, primary actions, confirmed verification markers, and structural anchors. Conveys deep institutional rigor.
- **Secondary (`#b45309` - Warm Amber/Ochre):** Reserved strictly for annotations, temporal discrepancies, attention-requiring fact-check flags, and archival alerts. Never used for pure decoration.
- **Tertiary (`#0f766e` - Deep Alpine Pine):** Deployed for verified botanical, historical preservation, or eco-transit integrity indicators.
- **Neutral (`#1e293b` - Slate Ink):** The fundamental ink color for body narrative, high-contrast headline displays, and prominent structural linework.
- **Canvas Base (`#fafaf9` - Warm Stone Off-White):** Provides a calm, glare-free ground plane reflecting natural unbleached rag paper.
- **Surface Elevation (`#ffffff`):** Pure white is reserved solely for raised inspection cards, contextual overlays, and editable data cells to delineate actionable fields against the canvas.
- **Border Neutral (`#e2e0dc` / `#cbd5e1`):** Subdued, razor-sharp perimeter lines that construct layout rhythm without visual weight.

## Typography

The typographic scale relies on **Plus Jakarta Sans** throughout all text treatments, balancing geometric precision with nuanced calligraphic cues in the terminals. This mirrors contemporary European print titles that maintain pristine digital legibility at small metadata scales.

### Typographic Principles
- **Optical Tracking:** Headlines carry subtle negative letter-spacing (`-0.025em` to `-0.015em`) for cohesive authority. Smaller metadata labels carry deliberate positive letter-spacing (`+0.02em` to `+0.04em`) to safeguard legibility in transit schedules and data tables.
- **Editorial Leading:** Body copy embraces open line height (`1.55` to `1.6`) to support uninterrupted deep-reading across multi-day itinerary analysis and bibliographic citations.
- **Data Pairs:** When numerical times (e.g., `08:45 CET`, `€14.50`) appear alongside locations, use `label-md` with tabular numerical alignment to sustain an orderly ledger cadence.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an intentional, asymmetric grid modeled after Swiss-style document layouts, ensuring data density remains legible alongside long-form itinerary critique.

### Grid Systems
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column asymmetric layout with a persistent 4-column verification sidebar (fact citations, transit validity, booking alerts) and an 8-column primary narrative timeline. Max container width capped at `1320px` to prevent fatigue.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout. The fact-checking panel collapses into contextual expanders pinned below each day’s itinerary schedule.
- **Mobile (Up to 767px):** Single-column stacked stream. Verification cards appear inline directly underneath contested itinerary events. Margins tighten to `1.25rem` to optimize horizontal data space.

### Spacing Cadence
- Content sections rely on structural divisions spaced at `space-xl` (2.5rem).
- Component clusters (such as an itinerary stop’s opening times, transit connections, and verification badge) maintain an internal rhythm governed by `space-sm` (0.5rem) and `space-md` (1rem).

## Elevation & Depth

The design system eliminates artificial drop shadows, floating halos, and blurred neomorphic layers. Spatial hierarchy is defined via **tonal stratification** and **crisp hairline borders**, creating an architectural, print-like quality.

### Elevation Strategy
- **Ground Floor (Base Canvas):** `#fafaf9` (Warm Stone). The pervasive foundational environment.
- **Level 1 (Card & Section Containers):** `#ffffff` with a `1px` border in `#e2e0dc`. Surfaces stand out via contrast against the warm off-white canvas rather than volumetric shadows.
- **Level 2 (Active Inspection Panels & Overlays):** `#ffffff` surrounded by a `1px` border in `#cbd5e1` accompanied by a micro ambient shadow (`0 2px 8px -2px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.04)`). The tint uses the neutral Slate Ink color to preserve natural light conditions.
- **Level 3 (Modal / Contextual Citations):** Suspended above a darkened veil (`rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.35)` with an ultra-fine backdrop diffusion) to center attention on critical verification conflicts.

## Shapes

The design system applies a **Soft (`1`)** shape logic. Radii are kept restrained to preserve the architectural, rectilinear discipline of high-end editorial page layouts.

### Radii Guidelines
- **Core Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Chips, Badges):** `0.25rem` (4px). Clean, subtle corners that feel tailored rather than rounded or toy-like.
- **Containers & Editorial Cards (`rounded-lg`):** `0.5rem` (8px). Softens larger surface edges while keeping a precise rectangular contour.
- **Overlays & Dialogs (`rounded-xl`):** `0.75rem` (12px). Applied strictly to major modal modules.
- **Prohibited:** Circular pills and bubble-style components are disallowed. Every structural element must harmonize with linear grid alignments.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep royal navy background (`#1e3a8a`), crisp `#ffffff` text, subtle 4px corner radius. No heavy shadows; active states transition down to `#172554`.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Off-white canvas with a `1px` border in `#cbd5e1`, slate ink text (`#1e293b`). Hover initiates a subtle tone shift to `#f1f5f9`.
- **Tertiary (Inline Editorial):** Text-only with underline offset by 3px, rendered in `#1e3a8a`, weight 600.

### Fact-Check Badges & Status Chips
- Badges use muted tints to prevent screen fatigue:
  - **Verified / Accurate:** Soft cream-sage background (`#f0fdf4`), deep pine text (`#166534`), hairline border (`#bbf7d0`).
  - **Disputed / Temporal Conflict:** Pale warm amber background (`#fffbeb`), rich ochre text (`#b45309`), hairline border (`#fde68a`).
  - **Historical / Route Flag:** Cool slate wash (`#f8fafc`), deep navy text (`#1e3a8a`), border (`#e2e8f0`).
- Content format: Small monospace-inspired label (`label-sm`), uppercase with slight letter tracking, paired with an understated status dot.

### Inputs & Search Fields
- Crisp white fill, border `1px` `#cbd5e1`, 4px corner radius.
- Focus state switches the border to `#1e3a8a` with an outline-free micro glow (`0 0 0 1px #1e3a8a`).
- Helper text and metadata use `body-sm` in `#64748b`.

### Cards & Itinerary Rows
- White container base with hairline borders (`#e2e0dc`).
- Left-hand editorial indicator border: 3px solid indicator in primary navy, secondary amber, or neutral slate reflecting the stop's fact-checked integrity.
- Padding follows `space-lg` internally, ensuring comfortable room between the event title, validation metadata, and supporting citation footnotes.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square with 2px corner radius (checkboxes) and disciplined rings (radios).
- Border in `#94a3b8`, checked state filled with `#1e3a8a` exhibiting clean white glyphs.

### Specialized Editorial Components
- **Citation Tooltip Drawer:** Inline textual references bracketed neatly `[Doc 1.2]` that reveal source links, archival records, and seasonal timetable validations without moving the reader off their timeline view.
- **Conflict Compare Module:** A split two-column micro-diff card highlighting the itinerary's claim versus verified archival/official reality side by side.
